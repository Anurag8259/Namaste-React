# Props Drilling problem in React:
        - Suppose you have 7-8 components , and all exist in a hierarchical form (two is a child of one and so on).
        - If you get data in ONE, and you need to pass that to EIGHT, You need to pass the data as props 
                from one to two , two to three and so on. (Even if two does not use the data)
        - Try to avoid Props Drilling
